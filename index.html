Privacy Policy for Orbit Balls

Effective Date: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Orbit Balls ("we", "us", or "our") handles user information when you play our mobile application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through transparent and minimal data handling practices.

1. Information We Collect and Why

Orbit Balls is designed to be enjoyed without creating a personal account or submitting sensitive credentials. We collect only the minimal technical information strictly required for core gameplay features:

  • Leaderboard & Score Data: To display player rankings and competition scores, the game transmits gameplay scores and optional display nicknames or anonymous player identifiers to our backend database.
  • Device & Diagnostics Information: Technical device identifiers, OS versions, and app performance logs may be automatically processed to ensure gameplay stability and bug tracking.

2. Third-Party Service Providers

We do not operate internal servers to store all data; instead, we partner with industry-standard third-party providers strictly to facilitate specific game services:

  • Supabase: We use Supabase infrastructure exclusively for storing and retrieving high scores and public leaderboard rankings. No private personal data, contact lists, or sensitive credentials are stored on Supabase. For more information, visit Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob: The application may display third-party advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may automatically collect and use device advertising identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID or IDFA), IP addresses, and cookies to serve personalized or contextual advertisements. You can manage or reset your advertising ID within your device settings. Learn more at Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with any third parties for commercial marketing or data brokerage purposes. Data transmitted to our service providers (Supabase and Google AdMob) is processed strictly to provide leaderboards and in-game advertisements as described above.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Orbit Balls does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information through the leaderboard or any other feature, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete the data from our records.

5. Data Retention and Security

We retain leaderboard scores only as long as necessary to maintain active competition rankings. We implement standard encryption and Row-Level Security (RLS) safeguards to prevent unauthorized access or tampering with public game data.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates, new integrations, or legal obligations. Any updates will be published directly on this web page with a revised effective date.
